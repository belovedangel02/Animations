Card Day 5

  • ⚠ Check this before setting out The Balon normally trades on Saturdays, and the bigger antiques edition, Gran Balon, on the second Sunday of each month — the second Sunday in August 2026 is the 9th, not the 23rd. On a plain Sunday the stalls are likely shut. Two easy fixes: swap this with the Saturday morning before Venaria (Aug 22), or replace it with the Porta Palazzo food market and a Quadrilatero vintage-shop wander.
